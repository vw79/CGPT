# 🌑 Nocturna

**Nocturna** is a **2.5D side-scrolling action-platformer** made in **Unity**. Built with 3D assets but featuring classic 2D movement, players explore a mysterious underground prison, battling monsters and bosses while upgrading their abilities to escape the darkness.

---

## 🔧 Features

- ⚔️ **Boss Battles**  
  Face off against powerful bosses, each with unique attack patterns and abilities.

- 💰 **Upgrade System**  
  Collect money from defeated enemies and spend it at angel statues to enhance your health, shield, or attack power.

- 🧭 **Level Progression**  
  Fight through dungeon levels, the black market, and the dark colosseum — each introducing new challenges and mechanics.

- 🕹️ **2.5D Gameplay**  
  Enjoy classic side-scrolling action with modern 3D visuals.

- 🧠 **Strategic Resource Management**  
  Choose how to spend your earnings to prepare for increasingly deadly challenges.

- 🧩 **Environmental Traps**  
  Avoid spikes, trap balls, falling stones, and time-based hazards.

- 🛡️ **Stat & Ability Growth**  
  Grow stronger over time by investing in upgrades and learning enemy patterns.
